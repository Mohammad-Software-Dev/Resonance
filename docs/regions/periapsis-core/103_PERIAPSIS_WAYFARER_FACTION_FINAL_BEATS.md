# Periapsis — Wayfarer and Faction Final Beats

**Purpose:** Give Mara, Ily, Jun and Oren meaningful conclusions while keeping every character playable and non-mandatory.

# Core rule

No character-specific finale mechanic is required.

Character identity appears through:

- dialogue;
- reaction;
- optional interaction;
- animation;
- epilogue framing.

The player never needs “the correct protagonist” for an ending.

# Mara Venn — Engineering without ownership

## Arc question

Can understanding a system justify controlling it?

Mara began as the most system-oriented Wayfarer.

Periapsis forces her to distinguish:

- knowing how to stabilize something;
- deciding what it should become.

## Final beat

Before Configuration Forum, Mara explains the technical feasibility of each available path without selecting one.

Her final branch reactions:

### Historical Continuity

Respects the engineering certainty but recognizes the cost of treating historical design as neutral.

### Distributed Meridian

Accepts lower efficiency as the price of reducing single-point control.

### Open Resonance

Fascinated and uneasy about moving from operator to participant in a system partly outside human comprehension.

### Bounded Accord

Sees the elegance and the maintenance burden; understands that good engineering can deliberately preserve limits.

## Epilogue direction

Mara remains involved in infrastructure, but her role changes from “fixer” to one of several stewards.

# Ily Sare — Life, adaptation and limits

## Arc question

Does adaptation deserve preservation simply because it exists?

Ily has consistently defended transformed ecologies and nontraditional life.

The campaign also shows that adaptation can:

- displace people;
- consume resources;
- become dangerous.

## Final beat

Ily argues that the final decision should account for living systems without romanticizing them.

### Historical Continuity

Mourns lost adaptations but may acknowledge restored survival capacity.

### Distributed Meridian

Supports regional ecological autonomy while recognizing some isolated habitats will still fail.

### Open Resonance

Treats Tessel as a life/agency question but remains cautious about assuming shared values.

### Bounded Accord

Accepts boundaries as a form of coexistence rather than total preservation.

## Epilogue direction

Ily becomes a mediator/researcher between human infrastructure and adapted ecologies.

# Jun Vale — Cooperation over spectacle

## Arc question

Is mastery about individual brilliance or creating possibilities for others?

Jun's early identity emphasizes speed, improvisation and risk.

Co-op traversal repeatedly reframes skill as:

- catching;
- timing;
- trusting;
- making routes for teammates.

## Final beat

Jun's Periapsis moments focus on coordinated execution rather than heroic solo action.

### Historical Continuity

Values restored mobility but notices who loses routes.

### Distributed Meridian

Sees a world of smaller, varied travel networks requiring cooperation.

### Open Resonance

Responds to unfamiliar shared-field movement with excitement and caution.

### Bounded Accord

Recognizes that boundaries can create routes instead of only restrictions.

## Epilogue direction

Jun becomes a route-builder/trainer rather than merely a runner.

# Oren Kade — Institutional memory and responsibility

## Arc question

Can institutions preserve safety without becoming incapable of admitting their own assumptions?

Oren has direct knowledge of Custodian doctrine.

He understands both:

- why emergency centralization exists;
- how it can erase local reality.

## Final beat

Oren is the clearest character to articulate that there is no politically neutral technical configuration.

### Historical Continuity

Recognizes much of the system he was trained to trust, while warning that governance must not pretend restored infrastructure restores the old social world.

### Distributed Meridian

Accepts the loss of centralized certainty.

### Open Resonance

Confronts the limits of Custodian categories for defining “system,” “life” and “operator.”

### Bounded Accord

Sees it as a deliberately constrained institution rather than a compromise that pleases everyone.

## Epilogue direction

Oren's future depends on earlier choices:

- reformist Custodian role;
- independent safety auditor;
- regional technical mediator.

# Custodian Assembly finale role

The Assembly remains internally divided after Meridian Custodian.

Possible final participation:

- provide diagnostics;
- oppose Open Resonance;
- support Historical Continuity;
- reluctantly assist Bounded Accord if technical guarantees exist;
- help Distributed Meridian prevent catastrophic shutdown even if they reject the strategy.

They do not disappear because the boss was defeated.

# Tessel finale role

Tessel communicates through:

- field structure;
- repeated signal grammar;
- distributed responses.

Avoid turning Tessel into a fully voiced human-like character at the last moment.

## Historical Continuity

Tessel may withdraw, fragment into bounded regions or become difficult to detect.

## Distributed Meridian

Tessel remains one distributed participant among many regional states, with uneven access.

## Open Resonance

Tessel becomes explicitly integrated into regulation.

The game still does not prove whether it is one mind or many.

## Bounded Accord

Tessel retains bounded channels and can negotiate across bridges, but some manifestations resist partitioning.

# Regional factions

Epilogue draws from prior region outcomes.

Examples:

- Orchard ecologists/farmers;
- Foundry civic/industrial groups;
- Observatory residents and basin researchers;
- Rust salvagers/archivists;
- Choir residents/mediators;
- Loom workers/engineers.

Do not show them all agreeing.

# Co-op final character presentation

Active player characters receive contextual lines.

Inactive named Wayfarers can still appear through staging/radio where narrative logic allows.

Guest players:

- see their character's final reaction;
- participate in advisory vote;
- receive completion credit.

# Dialogue density

Before commit:

- concise;
- information-first;
- no 10-minute council cutscene.

After commit:

- emotional beats can breathe;
- credits threshold remains player-controlled.

# Acceptance criteria

- each Wayfarer has a clear final thematic beat;
- no character gates a configuration;
- Custodians remain credible after defeat;
- Tessel remains non-human in presentation;
- regional factions visibly disagree;
- co-op guests receive meaningful finale presence.
