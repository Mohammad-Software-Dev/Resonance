# Final Configurations — Prerequisites, Tradeoffs and Epilogue Rules

**Purpose:** Lock exact finale availability logic and consequence framing.

# Design rules

- No ending is called good, bad, true or canon.
- No standard ending depends on one missable collectible.
- Missing prerequisites are visible before final commit.
- Earlier regional choices influence cost/feasibility but rarely hard-lock a whole philosophy by themselves.
- Host confirms the irreversible choice; guests vote/advice and fully participate in mechanics.

# Historical Continuity

## Availability

Always available after Anchor Zero reaches Neutral Zero.

## Technical advantage conditions

High Power Reserve, strong Structural Access and Maximum Harvest make this path easier/safer mechanically.

They are advantages, not requirements.

## Core outcome

Anchor Zero resumes centralized coordination.

## Gains

- highest conventional system coherence;
- many historical routes/services recover;
- Custodian safety models regain relevance;
- orbital correction becomes predictable.

## Losses

- Tessel expression is restricted;
- some Phase/field routes disappear;
- regional adaptations incompatible with master timing are narrowed or abandoned;
- central governance gains more control over local systems.

## Epilogue themes

- stability;
- restored connectivity;
- resentment/relief over standardization;
- Tessel becomes quiet, bounded or distant depending prior state.

# Distributed Meridian

## Availability

Always available after Anchor Zero reaches Neutral Zero.

## Technical advantage conditions

Distributed Lift, strong regional support and adaptation-friendly states reduce cascade difficulty.

Not requirements.

## Core outcome

Master control is permanently dispersed among regional Anchors.

## Gains

- transformed communities/ecologies retain more autonomy;
- no single master Anchor can overwrite regional state;
- regional systems can continue evolving independently.

## Losses

- some high-capacity central transit/industry stays offline forever;
- sectors may remain physically isolated or lower-power;
- large-scale correction requires cooperation between regions.

## Epilogue themes

- regional autonomy;
- uneven recovery;
- new interdependence;
- permanent loss of parts of historical Meridian.

# Open Resonance

## Availability

Requires **Viable Tessel Channel**.

A viable channel exists if any one condition is true:

- Choir outcome = Open Chorus;
- Choir outcome = Partitioned Accord;
- Carrier Lock was chosen but a bounded channel was restored through late Tessel/Crown quest work.

The Configuration Forum explicitly names the missing condition when unavailable.

## Core outcome

Tessel becomes a recognized participant in Meridian regulation.

## Gains

- field networks can stabilize phenomena humans could not centrally model;
- Phase infrastructure becomes more integrated;
- Tessel communication expands.

## Losses

- humans no longer have exclusive control;
- historical validation guarantees no longer fully apply;
- opt-out communities lose some integrated network services;
- Tessel's long-term development remains uncertain.

## Epilogue themes

- cooperation under uncertainty;
- new forms of communication;
- political resistance;
- unanswered questions about identity and agency.

# Bounded Accord

## Availability

Requires all four support classes.

### A. Controlled Field Channel

One of:

- Choir Partitioned Accord;
- Open Chorus + completed Safe Band / mediation work;
- Carrier Lock + late restored bounded channel.

### B. Distributed Support Capacity

One of:

- Loom Distributed Lift;
- or at least three qualified regional support/calibration flags across Orchard, Foundry, Observatory, Rust Sea and Loom support quests.

### C. Alignment Reserve

One of:

- Astral Shepherd defeated;
- or three regional Anchor calibration diagnostics completed.

### D. Custodian Interface Access

Available through mandatory Crown resolution unless the player intentionally destroys diagnostic access in a future narrative revision. Version 1 assumes it remains available.

## Core outcome

Human conventional control, Tessel field control and regional Anchor autonomy are separated into bounded partitions.

Anchor Zero loses master authority permanently.

## Gains

- no single system controls the entire Meridian;
- Tessel communication survives;
- regional autonomy survives;
- conventional infrastructure remains in bounded sectors.

## Permanent losses

- Anchor Zero can never return to full master control;
- maximum correction reserve decreases;
- some high-output historical systems are retired;
- partition maintenance becomes a permanent civic burden;
- several factions consider the outcome unacceptable.

## Why this is not a golden ending

It preserves more relationships but sacrifices:

- centralized emergency capacity;
- future restoration potential;
- simplicity;
- energy/output headroom.

Its long-term success depends on continuing cooperation.

# Ending record

Completion profile records:

- configuration completed;
- world-state snapshot hash/version;
- optional mastery flags;
- difficulty/accessibility metadata only for personal record, not reward eligibility.

# Epilogue generation

Epilogue selects 4–6 regional cards/scenes based on:

- final configuration;
- region outcome;
- major support quest;
- faction/Tessel state.

Do not attempt to show every choice.

Prioritize:

1. one scene showing the configuration's primary gain;
2. one scene showing its primary cost;
3. 2–3 major regional consequences;
4. one Tessel/Custodian consequence;
5. Wayfarer closing state.

# No hidden morality score

Epilogue logic reads explicit choices and derived technical state.

It does not summarize the player as compassionate, authoritarian, selfish, etc.

# Replay

After credits, Continue returns to the pre-commit PC15/16 state with completion record retained.

Player may pursue missing prerequisites and complete another configuration.

# Acceptance criteria

- prerequisites are explicit;
- standard paths remain broadly available;
- Open Resonance has a recoverable path after Carrier Lock;
- Bounded Accord has multiple prerequisite routes;
- each ending contains a real permanent cost;
- epilogues show consequences without moral scoring.
