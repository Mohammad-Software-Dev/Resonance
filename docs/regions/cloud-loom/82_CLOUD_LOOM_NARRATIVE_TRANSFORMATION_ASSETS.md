# Cloud Loom — Narrative, Transformation, Art, Audio and Asset Requirements

**Purpose:** Ground Cloud Loom in labor, orbital survival, extraction and visible late-game stakes.

# Narrative function

Cloud Loom asks:

> **How much extraction is justified when the whole habitat is in danger—and who bears the risk of keeping the system alive?**

The player can now see the full orbital context.

This region should make prior abstract warnings concrete.

# Loom society

Cloud Loom communities include:

- kite crews;
- atmospheric engineers;
- route captains;
- pressure technicians;
- orbital analysts;
- families living in service rings.

Their culture values route prediction, tether discipline, mutual rescue, weather/current literacy and redundancy.

They are not reckless “sky people.” Their expertise keeps the Meridian supplied.

# Primary NPCs

## Asha Renn — Route Captain

Practical regional guide.

Supports safe operation but knows reduced output has consequences elsewhere.

## Cor Vale — Atmospheric Engineer

Understands harvest capacity and orbital support.

## Dima Kest — Worker Representative

Represents people exposed to current/tether failures.

Pushes against treating human risk as a spreadsheet value.

## Sen Orr — Orbital Analyst

Connects Loom output to Periapsis decay.

Provides quantitative consequence framing.

## Lio Marr — Kite Mechanic

Supports Invert acquisition.

Treats relationship reversal as powerful but dangerous maintenance technique.

# Regional choices

## Maximum Harvest

Local:

- stronger currents;
- high machinery activity;
- worker zones contract;
- high resource throughput.

External:

- more late power/resources;
- Periapsis has greater conventional reserve;
- Broken Transit Crown machinery more active.

## Stable Envelope

Local:

- calmer current network;
- safer worker habitats;
- reduced throughput.

External:

- less conventional reserve;
- some late infrastructure weaker;
- lower storm/exterior hazard.

## Distributed Lift

Local:

- medium output;
- complex multi-lane routing;
- some unstable sectors remain.

External:

- selected stabilization nodes/regions supported;
- more distributed late traversal options;
- no maximum single-system reserve.

# Cross-region interactions

## Choir

- Open Chorus may use Loom structures as carrier.

## Rust Sea

Directed Migration destination may alter ballast/load requirements.

Relief Diversion may increase external support demand.

# Music

Palette:

- broad sustained strings/winds;
- cable-like metallic rhythm;
- open harmonic space;
- stronger forward pulse during long traversal.

Avoid generic heroic sky music.

# Production constraints

- no general atmospheric fluid simulation;
- distant clouds are presentation;
- cloth not collision truth;
- huge vistas use aggressive LOD/culling;
- current VFX scale independently from collision;
- long routes stream in controlled clusters.

# Acceptance criteria

Players understand:

- why Cloud Loom matters to survival;
- who benefits from high output;
- who bears its risk;
- why lower output is not consequence-free;
- how prior choices changed demand;
- how Loom outcome shapes late-game conditions.
