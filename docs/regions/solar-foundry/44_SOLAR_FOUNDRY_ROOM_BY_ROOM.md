# Solar Foundry — Room-by-Room Production Specification

**Region ID:** SF  
**Coverage:** Foundry Threshold → Manual Works → Upper Heliostat → Glass Regent → regional power decision  
**Target first-play duration:** 2.5–3.5 hours critical route; 4–5 hours exploratory  
**Purpose:** Greybox-ready production layout

## Global rules

- Critical route is solo-completable.
- Human co-op replaces Echo in simultaneous-actor interactions.
- Extra players receive routing, defense or relay roles rather than decorative switches.
- Beam mechanics are introduced safely before lethal combinations.
- Heat hazards never depend on lighting alone.
- Mirror/glass states use shape/pattern as well as color.
- Critical beam objects reset cleanly.
- Vector Shift is safely taught before mandatory use.
- Glass Regent retry remains under 20 seconds.

# Foundry Threshold

## SF01 — Sunward Transfer

**Purpose:** Establish Foundry identity before complexity.

Gameplay:

- standard traversal;
- one inactive mirror assembly;
- one low-intensity beam over a harmless lane;
- one fixed-anchor route.

Narrative:

- Foundry is running at a fraction of historical output;
- workers have intentionally blocked several automatic alignment commands.

Vista:

- gas giant dominates the lower sky;
- distant heliostat petals track sunlight;
- several mirrors remain frozen off-angle.

Reachable before Orchard completion.

---

## SF02 — Shadow Intake

**Purpose:** Teach beam hazard/tool language.

A shutter cycles:

- shadow;
- low beam;
- high beam.

Player learns:

- beam path telegraph;
- occlusion;
- glass baffle blocks a beam;
- Repel can reposition a light baffle.

First cycle cannot kill.

---

## SF03 — Glazier Walk

**Purpose:** Introduce Glazier.

Learning:

- Glazier forms brittle shield;
- attacks crack attachment;
- Attract removes loose shield;
- detached shield blocks one beam;
- Repel turns it into projectile.

Optional shelf rewards early use of shield placement.

---

## SF04 — Prism Relay

**Purpose:** First redirected-projectile problem.

Prism Drone fires slow optical bolt.

Player may:

- dodge;
- Repel directly;
- redirect through fixed mirror into receiver.

The receiver opens the route.

Enemy fire is established as infrastructure.

---

## SF05 — Kiln Quarter

**Purpose:** Safe social cluster.

Services:

- Relay/save;
- map;
- loadout;
- fabrication/shop;
- party management.

NPCs:

- Nera Sol — master mirrorwright;
- Vey Ardan — furnace archivist;
- Pell Rusk — shift supervisor;
- Senn Vale — lens technician;
- workers/apprentices.

Critical story:

- full historical alignment could restore enormous power;
- it would also overheat adapted lower districts and reactivate old systems.

Players without Latch/Orbit can reach the Quarter but cannot complete the upper route.

# Manual Works

## SF06 — Quench Channel

**Purpose:** Teach environmental Heat modifiers.

Hot glass channel crosses room.

Cooling vents create windows.

Rules:

- hot zones slow Heat cooling;
- quench zones accelerate cooling;
- no second thermal meter.

Heat Leech introduced with low failure pressure.

---

## SF07 — Shield Works

**Purpose:** Glass-state tutorial.

Glass cycles through:

1. Molten;
2. Forming;
3. Solid;
4. Cracked;
5. Shattered.

Player uses glass as:

- cover;
- temporary platform;
- mirror;
- projectile.

---

## SF08 — Prism Courtyard

**Purpose:** First true beam-routing room.

Elements:

- source;
- mirror A;
- shutter;
- receiver;
- Prism Drone.

### Solo

Echo maintains mirror A while player reaches shutter.

### 2P

One player mirror, one shutter.

### 3P

Third redirects hostile fire into a secondary receiver.

### 4P

Two parallel lanes:

- pair 1 maintains primary beam;
- pair 2 manages drone + secondary mirror.

Result opens shortcut toward SF05.

---

## SF09 — Furnace Ram Track

**Purpose:** Introduce Furnace Ram.

Ram charges along industrial rail.

Direct Repel does not stop it.

Solutions:

- switch track;
- move mirror machinery into lane;
- bait Ram into heat baffle.

Lesson: Heavy targets are manipulated through systems.

---

## SF10 — Glass Canal

**Purpose:** Forming geometry traversal.

Automated arms create temporary glass plates.

Player chains:

- Latch;
- forming platform;
- Repel;
- Orbit around support.

Advanced route reaches a Conduit-oriented Module.

---

## SF11 — Vector Crucible

**Purpose:** Acquire **Vector Shift**.

Fiction:

Old inspection crews used a field coupler to redirect themselves between heliostat structures.

Tutorial:

1. launch toward anchor;
2. Vector Shift once toward second anchor;
3. Shift after Latch release;
4. Shift to safe landing;
5. optional Shift + Repel speed route.

Prototype rule:

- one Shift per airborne Resonance chain;
- reset on stable ground or authored reset anchor;
- no unlimited steering.

Personal ability; no shared state.

---

## SF12 — Refraction Shaft

**Purpose:** First mandatory Vector Shift traversal.

Beam shutters create alternating safe corridors.

Player combines:

- Latch;
- Vector Shift;
- moving mirror anchor.

Recovery ledges on first half.

---

## SF13 — Marshal Approach

**Purpose:** Foreshadow Kiln Marshal.

Environment shows:

- stripped mirror armor;
- broken inspection rails;
- heat scars at several attack angles.

Marshal visible before arena.

---

## SF14 — Kiln Marshal Arena

**Elite:** Kiln Marshal.

Reward:

- Foundry Grid Key;
- capacity/Core progression component;
- access to Upper Heliostat.

Fast lift shortcut opens back to SF05.

# Upper Heliostat

## SF15 — Heliostat Lift

**Purpose:** Scale reveal.

Huge mirror carriage carries party upward.

Mechanics:

- moving anchors;
- beam occlusion;
- safe Vector Shift practice.

Players ride independently on separate carriage surfaces.

---

## SF16 — Split Spectrum Bridge

**Purpose:** Signature multi-actor puzzle.

Bridge exists only while two receiver lanes remain powered.

### Solo

Echo holds mirror A.

Player routes mirror B and Vector Shifts to final latch before playback ends.

### 2P

One route each.

### 3P

Third clears Heat Leech/Prism pressure and stabilizes temporary glass.

### 4P

Two pairs operate both lanes, swapping routing/crossing responsibilities halfway.

No one remains permanently on a switch.

---

## SF17 — Lensworks Convergence Chamber

Optional mastery challenge.

Stages:

1. redirect enemy projectile through two mirrors;
2. Vector Shift across rotating shutters;
3. maintain Link/Echo relationship while carrying brittle reflector.

Reward choice:

- **Prism Return** — reflected projectile retains more directional control;
- **Cold Circuit** — perfect Resonance action briefly improves Heat cooling;
- **Glass Memory** — temporary glass remains stable slightly longer.

---

## SF18 — Shadow Furnace

**Purpose:** Narrative pacing valley.

A lower-output worker district is deliberately kept in shadow.

Environmental story proves manual misalignment is a survival practice, not negligence.

Optional **Shadow Ledger** research begins/completes here and helps unlock Fractured Spectrum calibration.

---

## SF19 — Solar Crown Exterior

**Purpose:** Spectacle traversal.

Players cross heliostat backs.

Mechanics:

- moving mirror anchors;
- Vector Shift;
- periodic orbital shadow;
- long launch lines;
- Link rescue opportunities.

Background mirrors are cosmetic/parametric unless tagged interactable.

## SF20 — Crown Routing Gallery

**Purpose:** Teach Regent grammar before boss.

No core boss rule appears first in the boss arena.

# Foundry Reconfiguration

## SF23 — Solar Distribution Control

### A — Historical Grid Alignment

- main furnaces power;
- heavy doors open;
- security constructs reactivate;
- lower shadow district partially evacuates.

### B — Distributed Civic Routing

- worker services improve;
- safe settlement routes power;
- large industrial systems remain partially dormant.

### C — Fractured Spectrum Calibration

- multiple lower-intensity beam paths appear;
- adapted glass habitat survives;
- advanced traversal mirrors remain;
- full industrial output unavailable.

Host confirms after guest advisory vote.
