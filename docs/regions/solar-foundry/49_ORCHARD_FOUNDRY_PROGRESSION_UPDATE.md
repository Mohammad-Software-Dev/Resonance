# Early Campaign Progression Update — Gravity Orchard + Solar Foundry

**Purpose:** Reconcile the now-detailed first two major regions with the global ability/progression graph.

# Locked early sequence

## Wayfarer Scar / Transit

Player receives:

- Run / Jump / Evade;
- Attract;
- Repel;
- Link in multiplayer;
- Echo in solo;
- first Core/Module frame;
- first Relay access.

## Gravity Orchard

Primary new movement:

- **Latch**

After The Pendulum / first major Guardian:

- **Orbit** becomes available as the next movement refinement.

Gravity Orchard proves:

- moving anchors;
- dual-actor Link/Echo;
- local gravity;
- regional world transformation.

## Solar Foundry

Primary new major movement:

- **Vector Shift**

Foundry proves:

- energy routing;
- projectile redirection;
- Heat pressure;
- temporary glass geometry;
- one intentional airborne line change.

# Updated timing targets

Typical first play:

- Attract: 5–10 min;
- Repel: 10–15 min;
- Link/Echo: 20–30 min;
- Latch: 1–2 h;
- Orbit: 2.5–4 h;
- Vector Shift: 5–7 h;
- Flux Field: 7–10 h;
- Phase: 10–14 h;
- Invert: 14–18 h.

Exploration and sequence breaks may shift hours.

# Campaign choice structure

After Transit Spine opens:

- Gravity Orchard is the recommended first full Guardian route;
- Solar Foundry outskirts can be explored early;
- deep Foundry assumes Latch and strongly benefits from Orbit.

A skilled player may push farther earlier.

Rules:

- no critical progression assumes Vector Shift before Latch;
- early Foundry exploration must not corrupt quest/save state;
- dialogue adapts to Orchard resolution state.

# Why Vector Shift belongs in Foundry

Foundry geometry naturally teaches directional redirection.

## Traversal

- switch line between mirror anchors;
- escape beam lanes;
- redirect after Orbit release.

## Combat

- redirect momentum around Glazier shield;
- reach Prism Drone;
- recover from Furnace Ram lane.

## Puzzle

- cross split-beam gates;
- move from one routed lane to another.

## Boss

- Glass Regent Phase 3;
- moving petals;
- final opposing alignment.

# Vector Shift rules

## Baseline

One Shift per valid airborne Resonance chain.

## Reset

- stable ground;
- authored reset anchor;
- Relay/safe platform.

## No free reset from

- enemy touch;
- tiny ledge scrape;
- repeated wall contact.

## Future upgrades

Candidates only:

- **Combat Reset** — stability break refreshes Shift under cooldown;
- **Team Relay** — successful catch may restore teammate Shift;
- **Precision Shift** — smaller correction preserves more speed.

Do not unlock all in Foundry.

# Core availability

By end of Foundry, target four meaningful Core families:

- Vector;
- Breaker;
- Weaver;
- **Conduit**.

## Conduit Core

Identity:

- projectile/energy routing;
- reflect timing;
- beam interaction.

Prototype baseline:

- reflected projectiles retain slightly more control;
- selected receiver/energy actions create a small Heat advantage.

Core technique candidate:

**Return Arc** — a well-timed reflected projectile briefly becomes a stronger Resonance target or gains one controlled redirection.

It must not trivialize Prism Drone or Regent routing.

# Foundry Module candidates

## Prism Return

Reflected projectile keeps more directional control.

## Cold Circuit

Perfect Resonance action briefly improves cooling.

## Glass Memory

Temporary glass remains stable longer.

## Heat Sink Weave

Reduces recovery penalty at high Heat if such a penalty survives prototype.

## Split Focus

Faster lock transition between two recently used mirror targets.

## Anneal Strike

Heavy attack gains stability damage against Hot/Annealed brittle components.

## Shadow Step

Leaving Solar Focus gives short cooling burst.

## Return Lens

Repelled projectile routed through valid mirror gains modest stability impact.

No Module is mandatory for critical path.

# Cross-region consequences

## Orchard → Foundry

### Historical Orchard

- heavier machinery power request;
- one conventional receiver route may activate earlier.

### Adaptive Orchard

- lower conventional load;
- alternate mirror route remains.

### Hybrid Orchard

- optional routing data contributes to Spectrum research.

These are meaningful touches, not separate region versions.

## Foundry → Orchard

### Historical Foundry

- irrigation machinery powers;
- one dangerous maintenance system activates.

### Civic Foundry

- settlement services improve;
- less industrial machinery.

### Spectrum Foundry

- selected Resonant plant/field phenomena appear.

# Gate inventory after Foundry

Player should recognize future blockers for:

- Flux-capable inert lattice;
- Phase barrier;

Map remembers blocker type after inspection.

# Sequence-break policy

Vector Shift may enable:

- collectible;
- shortcut;
- optional challenge;
- early side room.

Do not allow it to:

- skip required tutorial;
- bypass irreversible choice trigger;
- enter late boss without quest state;
- corrupt save progression.

# Multiplayer progression mismatch

Guest lacking Vector Shift in a post-Foundry host world receives a session license when required.

Guest who owns Vector Shift in a pre-Foundry host world may be constrained only at protected gates where sequence breaking would corrupt first-play flow.

Do not globally remove advanced movement in harmless areas.

# Echo + Vector Shift

Echo may replay a recorded Vector Shift trajectory.

Echo does not dynamically choose a new target during playback.

Recorded TargetIDs remain authoritative.

# Learning curve

**Route systems.**

This is the intended early mastery progression.

# Acceptance criteria

- Latch/Orbit remain useful after Vector Shift;
- Vector Shift expands rather than replaces movement skill;
- Foundry can be partially explored early;
- Conduit adds build variety without being required;
- progression mismatch works through existing session-license rules;
- no new ability forces a network architecture rewrite.
