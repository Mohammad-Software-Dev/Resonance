# Optional Mastery and Superboss Placement Review

**Purpose:** Place late optional encounters so they reward mastery without distorting critical-path balance or making the finale depend on completionist content.

## Principles

Optional late-game encounters may assume:

- full foundational toolkit;
- advanced movement knowledge;
- meaningful build choices;
- stronger co-op coordination;
- tighter execution.

They may not be required for:

- basic Periapsis access;
- a fair critical-path build;
- ordinary ending access unless a clearly signposted special/hidden ending explicitly depends on optional completion.

## Broken Transit Crown placements

### Astral Shepherd

**Placement:** BTC22–24 optional branch.

**Role:** optional major systems boss.

**Reward:**

- Periapsis Alignment Reserve;
- high-tier Module;
- alternate Crown route;
- Anchor Zero diagnostic.

**Rule:** useful in finale, never mandatory.

### Convergence Prototype

**Placement:** BTC35.

**Role:** ultimate pre-finale Echo/co-op mastery chamber.

Tests:

- Flux + Phase + Invert composition;
- Link/Echo responsibility;
- moving anchors;
- route recovery.

Reward:

- cosmetic/title;
- challenge record;
- flexible Module component.

Avoid exclusive best-in-slot power.

### Broken Relay Swarm

**Placement:** BTC15.

**Role:** optional mid-Crown mini-boss.

Reward:

- shortcut;
- late resource;
- Relay lore.

## Existing optional major/superboss candidates

### The Unmoored

Recommended placement:

- late Phase/Transit fault route;
- accessible after Choir + Crown progress;
- not inside Periapsis critical path.

Identity:

- extreme state-transition movement test.

### Tessel Fragment: The Many-Voiced Gate

Recommended placement:

- late Choir/Core branch;
- availability/outcome depends on Tessel relationship.

May resolve through:

- combat;
- negotiated shutdown;
- redirected hostile traces.

Do not require it for ordinary finale.

### Obsolete Anchor Prototype

The previously working-named “Pale Engine” requires final rename before production.

Recommended placement:

- hidden Foundry/Spine junction;
- late return after Flux/Phase/Invert.

Identity:

- learns repeated movement routes;
- locks overused anchors;
- rewards improvisation.

This is a strong optional anti-habit challenge but should remain outside Crown's required Custodian counterplay.

## Difficulty bands

### Critical bosses

Target:

- challenging;
- readable;
- 3–10 learning attempts depending late-game encounter;
- granular accessibility.

### Optional major bosses

Target:

- 6–15 attempts for median late-game action player;
- more demanding route composition;
- shorter/cleaner retry loop.

### Superbosses

May demand:

- highly advanced movement;
- strong build understanding;
- deeper coordination.

Still preserve:

- visual fairness;
- accessibility unless a standardized challenge leaderboard explicitly opts into fixed rules;
- fast reset.

## Reward policy

Optional hard content rewards should emphasize:

- expression;
- prestige;
- flexibility;
- alternate support.

Good:

- cosmetics;
- titles;
- lore;
- unique but not mandatory Modules;
- Periapsis support modifier;
- alternate route;
- challenge record.

Avoid:

- huge raw damage advantage;
- mandatory survivability;
- unique ability required for hidden critical-path mechanics.

## Co-op scaling

Optional mastery should not assume 4P only.

### Solo

Echo-supported solution authored intentionally.

### 2P

direct paired mechanics.

### 3P

pair + flex.

### 4P

parallel pairs/subsystems.

Difficulty may increase through simultaneous responsibilities rather than HP.

## Crown pacing

Recommended sequence:

- Anchor Knight — mandatory elite;
- Broken Relay Swarm — optional;
- Astral Shepherd — optional major;
- Convergence Prototype — optional mastery;
- Meridian Custodian — mandatory narrative climax.

This prevents boss fatigue.

## Periapsis rule

Periapsis may read optional flags and provide:

- easier stabilization window;
- additional support node;
- alternate route;
- special dialogue;
- hidden configuration prerequisite.

But the critical final route remains viable without optional mastery completion.

## Acceptance criteria

- Crown critical pacing is not boss-heavy;
- optional challenges feel valuable;
- no optional boss secretly becomes mandatory power;
- solo and 4P remain supported;
- reward inflation stays controlled;
- Periapsis can acknowledge mastery without depending on it.
