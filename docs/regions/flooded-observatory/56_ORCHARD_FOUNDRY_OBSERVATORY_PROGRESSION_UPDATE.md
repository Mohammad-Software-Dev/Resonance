# Mender Core

Identity:

- Fracture management;
- rescue;
- stabilization;
- recovery through skilled play.

Must work solo and co-op.

## Baseline effect candidate

A portion of successful high-skill recovery actions converts Fracture more efficiently.

Examples:

- perfect boundary exit near hazard;
- successful revive/rescue;
- stability break;
- selected defensive Resonance action.

Do not reward passive hiding.

## Technique candidate — Stabilize Link

A correctly timed Link rescue grants:

- brief knockback resistance;
- limited Fracture preservation;
- no invulnerability.

Solo equivalent:

- successful Echo-assisted recovery or self-stabilization action can trigger smaller benefit.

# Observatory Module candidates

## Boundary Memory

Briefly previews likely exit vector from current sphere.

Should not replace accessibility exit guide; build version is lighter/skill-oriented.

## Pressure Relief

Grounding a charged water volume cools a small amount of Heat.

## Wet Vector

Vector Shift inside WaterTransit preserves slightly more velocity.

## Safe Harbor

Entering water shortly after heavy knockback reduces recovery time.

## Lens Return

A projectile crossing a valid water-lens interaction gains modest stability effect.

## Rescue Current

Linking to an Unstable teammate across a water boundary acquires slightly faster.

## Quiet Surface

Leaving a water sphere without taking damage briefly reduces enemy tracking pressure.

## Basin Grip

Attract/Latch against a fixed anchor while inside high-flow water gains slightly improved stability.

No Module is required for critical progression.

# Water as progression without new button

Observatory creates progression through **knowledge**.

Early player:

- enters sphere cautiously;
- exits unpredictably.

Experienced player:

- uses entry angle;
- reads flow;
- preserves speed;
- intentionally brakes;
- chains water exit into Vector Shift/Orbit.

This supports the project's “mastery over grind” principle.

# Region-order dependencies

## Foundry power outcome → Observatory

### Historical Grid

- more Observatory pumps/pressure controls are powered;
- one route easier;
- one old research security system active.

### Distributed Civic

- Relay/camp infrastructure stronger;
- some heavy pumps remain manual.

### Fractured Spectrum

- selected water-lens interactions show Resonant anomalies;
- small Tessel/Choir foreshadow.

None creates a different region.

## Orchard outcome → Observatory relief logic

Orchard water need changes based on its Anchor state.

The Relief Diversion option must query that state.

This prevents contradictory storytelling.

# Observatory outcome → Orchard

### Historic Channel

- conventional water supply improves moderately;
- if Orchard needs it, irrigation becomes more reliable.

### Suspended Basin

- little external water benefit;
- unique research/ecology data may unlock later content.

### Relief Diversion

- strong external effect;
- Orchard or alternate target visibly changes.

# Observatory outcome → Transit

Historic or Relief outcomes may:

- activate pressure systems;
- open one water-driven lift;
- change a lower Transit room.

Suspended may:

- preserve alternate sphere link/shortcut through Observatory instead.

# Observatory → Rust Sea setup

The Observatory should foreshadow Flux without granting it.

Examples:

- inert structural lattice visible behind dry/flooded route;
- research log describes field activation of metallic particulates;
- one impossible-to-move metallic mechanism is marked for later.

At exit, player should understand:

> the next major limitation is not movement skill alone; some material simply does not respond yet.

This makes Flux desirable.
