# Flooded Observatory — Narrative, Transformation, Art, Audio and Asset Requirements

**Purpose:** Give the Observatory a social and thematic identity built around water allocation, research legacy and adapted habitats.

# Narrative function

Gravity Orchard asks whether adapted ecology should be preserved.

Solar Foundry asks who should receive recovered power.

Flooded Observatory asks:

> **Who owns a resource when infrastructure failure created a new ecosystem around it?**

Water is simultaneously:

- habitat;
- infrastructure;
- historical property;
- scientific resource;
- survival resource.

The region should make each of those claims understandable.

# Observatory community

The surviving population includes:

- researchers;
- descendants of technicians;
- residents living around dry channels;
- sphere-tenders maintaining suspended habitats;
- displaced families from failed lower districts.

Their culture is split between:

- restoring historic hydrology;
- preserving suspended basins;
- using Observatory water to help external settlements.

No group is purely selfish or enlightened.

# Primary NPCs

## Dr. Sava Rheel — Systems Hydrologist

Role:

- regional technical lead;
- understands original canals and current sphere network.

Traits:

- rigorous;
- impatient with romantic arguments unsupported by data;
- willing to accept ecological loss if human survival requires it;
- equally willing to reject restoration if it is technically wasteful.

Function:

- explains hydrology options;
- frames actual resource quantities and consequences.

## Mero Ahn — Cartographer of Volumes

Role:

- maps moving water habitats rather than fixed rooms.

Traits:

- quiet;
- spatially obsessive;
- skeptical of historic maps;
- respects Ily's ecological perspective.

Function:

- map upgrades;
- moving-sphere route clues;
- optional advanced traversal data.

## Tali Vennor — Displaced Resident

Role:

- represents people whose old district dried out after the fracture.

Traits:

- practical;
- not hostile to sphere ecology;
- angry that “adaptation” is sometimes used to dismiss people who lost basic infrastructure.

Function:

- makes Historic Channel option materially human.

## Hesh Cor — Volume Technician

Role:

- maintains containment fields and pressure collars.

Traits:

- physically courageous;
- improvisational;
- deeply familiar with Leviathan behavior.

Function:

- teaches moving-sphere hardware;
- helps unlock Relief Diversion engineering path.

## Eri Tal — Basin Ecologist

Role:

- studies life that evolved inside suspended spheres.

Traits:

- evidence-driven;
- protective of unique habitats;
- refuses to treat all current water life as sacred simply because it is new.

Function:

- supports Living Basins quest;
- documents which ecosystems can relocate and which cannot.

# Wayfarer perspectives

## Mara

Focuses on whether the three options are technically sustainable.

Arc beat:

- learns that “efficient allocation” still encodes value judgments about who bears disruption.

## Ily

Feels strong affinity with suspended ecosystems.

Complication:

- some sphere habitats are invasive or dependent on infrastructure that is failing elsewhere.

This avoids automatically validating preservation.

## Jun

Connects with technicians who travel by momentum through spheres.

Regional beat:

- improvisational movement culture validates Jun's skill while showing the danger of overconfidence.

## Oren

Recognizes old Custodian water-priority doctrine.

Can reveal:

- historic emergency priorities;
- previous forced water transfers;
- how institutional rules privileged central systems over peripheral settlements.

# Regional quests

## Main quest

1. reach Aperture Camp;
2. learn the original canals are dry;
3. restore access to Suspended Labs;
4. understand charged/moving spheres;
5. gain Mender Core access;
6. defeat Astrolabe Shell;
7. recover Deep Lens hydrology data;
8. confront Leviathan;
9. choose regional water configuration.

## Optional — Living Basins

Survey several sphere ecosystems.

Outcome:

- identifies habitats that can survive relocation;
- identifies one habitat that cannot;
- improves consequence visibility for Suspended vs Historic choices.

## Optional — Dry Names

Recover resident records from the old dry district.

Outcome:

- identifies families/communities displaced by water loss;
- unlocks new dialogue for Historic Channel choice.

## Optional — Relief Line

Trace an old transfer main toward Transit and an external failing settlement/agricultural system.

Outcome:

- proves Relief Diversion is technically viable;
- identifies who would benefit;
- reveals what local pressure capacity would be lost.

## Optional — Leviathan Study

Collect non-lethal observations before the boss.

Outcome:

- shows Leviathan is adapted wildlife, not a system guardian;
- may alter post-boss dialogue or survival outcome;
- should not remove the boss encounter entirely unless later narrative scope explicitly chooses that path.

# Regional choice framing

The decision UI reports known facts:

- water volume moved;
- districts affected;
- ecosystem impact;
- external benefit;
- infrastructure restored/lost.

Do not attach moral adjectives.

Guests vote; host confirms.

# Outcome A — Historic Channel Restoration

## Local

- large suspended spheres drain;
- old canals fill;
- lower city floods in controlled layers;
- upper research access stabilizes;
- some current sphere routes disappear;
- aquatic species migrate or decline.

## NPC

- Tali's community returns to some infrastructure;
- sphere specialists relocate work;
- Eri documents ecological loss/relocation;
- Mero's map changes substantially.

## External

- conventional water/pressure link improves Transit;
- Orchard may receive more predictable irrigation depending prior choices.

# Outcome B — Suspended Basin Stabilization

## Local

- sphere positions stabilize;
- containment failures reduce;
- advanced sphere routes remain;
- old lower canals stay mostly dry.

## NPC

- sphere communities/ecology remain;
- displaced historic-district residents remain dissatisfied or relocate;
- maintenance burden remains visible.

## External

- little surplus water leaves region;
- Transit/Orchard benefit less;
- future Resonance research gains access to stable suspended-volume data.

# Outcome C — Relief Diversion

## Local

- selected spheres shrink/drain;
- Observatory keeps enough water for survival but loses some research/habitat capacity;
- neither historic city nor sphere ecology is fully restored.

## External

Water reaches a specified failing system.

Recommended primary external consequence:

**Gravity Orchard irrigation**, because it creates a visible relationship between two already-known communities.

If Orchard state makes that need irrelevant, diversion may instead support:

- Wayfarer Scar;
- Transit pressure infrastructure;
- another defined settlement.

The game should never pretend the same relief target is critical regardless of previous decisions.

# Cross-region state logic

Relief target must query prior world state.

Example:

- if Orchard Historical restoration created high water demand → Orchard is primary relief target;
- if Orchard Adaptive preserved low-demand system → Transit/Scar may become stronger relief target;
- if previous state already solved a need → choice text updates honestly.

This is an important demonstration that regional decisions interact, rather than stacking independent moral menus.

# Landmark

**The Great Aperture**

A gigantic telescope/lens assembly that frames the gas giant and distant Meridian structure through multiple water volumes.

Visible from:

- FO01;
- FO05;
- FO17;
- FO21;
- selected Transit vista.

# Color/material strategy

Do not make Observatory simply “the blue level.”

Identity should come from:

- water lensing;
- pale ceramics;
- oxidized research metals;
- dry mineral stains;
- warm occupied interiors;
- high-contrast charged-water patterns.

# Audio

## Outside water

- structural groans;
- telescope motors;
- distant water movement;
- pressure valves;
- dripping isolated droplets.

## Inside water

Use filtered/muffled mix but retain:

- attack telegraphs;
- teammate ping;
- Link state;
- boss cues.

Do not prioritize realism over gameplay information.

## System cues

Distinct sounds for:

- boundary entry;
- boundary exit;
- charged volume;
- grounding success;
- pressure imbalance;
- moving sphere lock;
- water transfer complete.

# Music

Regional palette:

- glassy/aqueous resonances;
- low strings or winds;
- slow pulse derived from telescope motion;
- sparse organic motifs;
- filtered variants inside water.

World transformation may change orchestration:

- Historic: more ordered mechanical pulse;
- Suspended: more floating sustained harmony;
- Relief: bittersweet reduction / external motif integration.

# Animation

Need:

- bubble/water-adapted enemy locomotion;
- boundary-crossing effects;
- pressure collar motion;
- telescope rotation;
- dry/flooded state transitions;
- NPC movement adapted to spheres.

Wayfarer animation should remain recognizable inside water; do not replace with full swim animation set.

# Dialogue density

Critical path:

- roughly 3–5 minutes mandatory before boss/choice if optional conversations skipped.

The region may support deeper optional scientific/cultural dialogue without forcing it.

# Co-op narrative

- all active Wayfarers acknowledged;
- guest votes visible;
- character-specific reactions optional;
- no critical clue depends on one specific active Wayfarer.

# Production constraints

- water spectacle must scale independently from gameplay truth;
- transformations reuse room geometry where possible;
- no room requires bespoke fluid simulation;
- no expensive full-scene refraction on minimum setting;
- water surface/material LODs planned from first art pass.

# Acceptance criteria

Players should understand:

- why water is suspended;
- who benefits from current state;
- who lost something because of it;
- why Historic restoration is attractive;
- why Suspended preservation is attractive;
- who Relief Diversion helps and what Observatory sacrifices;
- how their prior regional decisions changed the choice context.
