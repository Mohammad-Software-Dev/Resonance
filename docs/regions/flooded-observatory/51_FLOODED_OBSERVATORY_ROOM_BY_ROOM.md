# Flooded Observatory — Room-by-Room Production Specification

**Region ID:** FO  
**Coverage:** Dry Periphery → Suspended Labs → Deep Lens Array → Leviathan Basin → regional water decision  
**Target first-play duration:** 2.5–3.5 hours critical route; 4–5 hours exploratory  
**Purpose:** Greybox-ready production layout

## Global rules

- Critical route is solo-completable.
- Water never replaces the core controller with free-swimming.
- Every water volume has a stable ID and reset state.
- Boundary, flow and charge are readable before lethal use.
- Solo Echo substitutes only one simultaneous actor.
- 3P/4P variants add parallel responsibility, not HP walls.
- No critical room depends on fluid simulation.
- Leviathan retry path remains under 20 seconds.

# Dry Periphery

## FO01 — Dry Approach

**Purpose:** Establish the contradiction: an Observatory full of water whose entrance is dry.

Gameplay:

- standard traversal;
- dry historic canal;
- one suspended water sphere visible through structure;
- one blocked pressure gate.

Narrative:

- old channels are empty;
- improvised pipes route small quantities to inhabited areas;
- research signage assumes normal gravity that no longer exists.

Vista:

- rotating telescope rings;
- multiple floating water spheres at different depths.

---

## FO02 — First Globe

**Purpose:** Safe water-boundary tutorial.

A small stable sphere sits between two platforms.

Player learns:

1. enter at low speed;
2. movement becomes damped;
3. exit preserves a readable fraction of velocity;
4. Repel near the boundary produces a stronger line change.

Failure drops to safe lower ledge.

---

## FO03 — Boundary Gallery

**Purpose:** Teach entry angle.

Three spheres:

- static;
- slow lateral flow;
- slow rotational flow.

Player crosses with no enemies.

Optional speed route rewards understanding rather than new ability.

---

## FO04 — Bubble Ray Nursery

**Purpose:** Introduce Bubble Ray.

One large sphere contains a Bubble Ray.

Player learns:

- enemy stays inside its water;
- moving/altering sphere position changes enemy's usable space;
- attacks/projectiles behave differently across boundary.

No lethal charge mechanics yet.

---

## FO05 — Aperture Camp

**Purpose:** First regional safe cluster.

Services:

- Relay;
- map;
- loadout;
- local research/fabrication service;
- party management.

NPCs:

- Dr. Sava Rheel — systems hydrologist;
- Mero Ahn — observatory cartographer;
- Tali Vennor — displaced resident;
- Hesh Cor — maintenance diver-equivalent/volume technician;
- optional visiting Keph.

Conflict introduced:

- historic channels could restore predictable infrastructure;
- current suspended spheres support ecology and settlements;
- another region/settlement is short of water.

# Suspended Labs

## FO06 — Pressure Hall

**Purpose:** Teach moving water volume.

A medium sphere is attached to a field collar.

Player manipulates collar to move sphere between two authored sockets.

Then uses sphere as traversal bridge.

### Solo

Direct interaction.

### Co-op

Players may ride while another repositions; no simultaneous requirement yet.

---

## FO07 — Lens Crab Lab

**Purpose:** Introduce Lens Crab.

Lens Crab uses shell optics to redirect a selected projectile.

Player can:

- attack around shell;
- use reflected projectile;
- move Crab into/out of water;
- exploit water-damped projectile timing.

---

## FO08 — Charged Reservoir

**Purpose:** Introduce Pressure Eel and charged water.

Sequence:

- Pressure Eel charges a sphere;
- grounding probe is visible;
- player moves probe/opens grounding path;
- discharged sphere becomes safe.

First charge cannot instantly kill.

---

## FO09 — Split Basin

**Purpose:** First multi-actor water problem.

Two spheres share one pressure regulator.

### Solo

Echo holds regulator while player crosses/moves second sphere.

### 2P

One controls regulator; one traverses.

### 3P

Third handles Bubble Ray/Pressure Eel pressure.

### 4P

Two pairs manage separate sphere lanes and exchange a pressure key/object.

Shortcut returns toward FO05.

---

## FO10 — Drowned Archive

**Purpose:** Refraction tutorial.

Water lens distorts an old archive corridor.

Player learns:

- visual distortion can mislead depth;
- Resonance target remains truthful;
- marked projectile follows authored refracted path.

Accessibility prompt may demonstrate low-distortion option without forcing it.

---

## FO11 — Siphon Works

**Purpose:** Introduce Siphon Colony.

Colony drains a small sphere and reforms another.

Player can:

- kill/support-interrupt;
- redirect its transfer;
- use transfer to create temporary route.

Narrative:

- some current water distribution is ecological rather than mechanical.

---

## FO12 — Surveyor Crossfire

**Purpose:** Introduce Drowned Surveyor.

Surveyor fires through water with altered travel speed/angle.

Room combines:

- one sphere;
- one dry lane;
- one moving cover panel.

Player learns to use boundary as projectile timing tool.

---

## FO13 — Mender Lab

**Purpose:** Regional build milestone.

Player obtains/accesses **Mender Core** family.

Mender identity:

- Fracture recovery;
- rescue;
- stabilization;
- safe aggression/support.

No movement gate depends on equipping it.

Optional tutorial shows:

- solo Fracture recovery benefit;
- co-op rescue stabilization benefit.

# Deep Lens Array

## FO14 — Orbital Pike Trench

**Purpose:** Introduce Orbital Pike.

Pike attacks across sphere boundary along authored strike path.

Counterplay:

- Attract/Repel knocks it off path;
- enter/exit sphere to change timing;
- Link rescue available.

---

## FO15 — Astrolabe Approach

**Purpose:** Foreshadow elite.

Large research rings rotate several small water masses around a central shell.

Environment teaches:

- moving sphere orbit;
- water can function as cover;
- pressure node changes orbit track.

---

## FO16 — Astrolabe Shell Arena

Elite encounter.

See dedicated enemy/elite specification.

## FO17 — Lens Array Ascent

**Purpose:** Advanced sphere-to-sphere movement.

Player uses:

- Vector Shift;
- water entry braking;
- exit momentum;
- moving sphere;
- Orbit around dry anchor.

Three difficulty routes:

- safe;
- combat;
- advanced collectible.

---

## FO18 — Triple Refraction Chamber

**Purpose:** Signature puzzle.

Three lenses and two water spheres route one calibration signal.

### Solo

Echo maintains one lens/regulator while player repositions second sphere and reaches receiver.

### 2P

One lens route each.

### 3P

Third handles charged-water timing.

### 4P

Two pairs operate separate signal paths which converge at final receiver.

No exact simultaneous frame requirement.

---

## FO19 — Pressure Gardens

**Purpose:** Pacing / ecology.

Non-hostile adapted aquatic life occupies stable spheres.

Environmental story:

- generations of life now depend on suspended water;
- historic restoration would relocate or destroy some habitats.

Optional **Living Basins** research begins here.

---

## FO20 — Dry City Overlook

**Purpose:** Show cost of current state.

Below the spheres lies an old residential/research district with dry cisterns and failed infrastructure.

NPC evidence shows:

- suspended-water preservation has a real opportunity cost;
- restoration has real beneficiaries.

This avoids making one outcome obviously virtuous.

---

## FO21 — Relay of Tides

**Purpose:** Pre-boss checkpoint and loadout.

Contains:

- Relay;
- local map;
- boss research;
- water-routing diagnostics.

Leviathan is revealed as an organism that grew across several isolated spheres and now physically links them.

# Regional Reconfiguration

## FO24 — Hydrology Control

Party chooses water outcome.

### A — Historic Channel Restoration

- major suspended spheres drain into historic canals;
- lower city floods in controlled way;
- upper telescope routes become conventional;
- aquatic life migrates.

### B — Suspended Basin Stabilization

- spheres remain;
- their positions become safer/more stable;
- sphere traversal persists;
- historic channels remain dry.

### C — Relief Diversion

Requires confirmed external need / optional quest state.

- water is routed through Transit infrastructure toward failing settlement/agriculture;
- local Observatory loses several spheres;
- external region receives major benefit.

Host confirms after guest vote.

---

- FO06 sphere disappears into channel;

- FO17 becomes partly dry platforming;
- FO19 habitats relocate;
- FO20 city becomes water-active.

## Suspended Basin

- sphere positions stabilize;
- FO17 keeps advanced water routes;
- FO19 expands;
- FO20 remains dry;
- new sphere shortcut appears.

- FO06/FO09 route geometry changes;

# Greybox acceptance

- all required rooms work solo;
- 2/3/4P variants valid;
- player predicts sphere exit after onboarding;
- no critical task requires arbitrary fluid behavior;
- no refraction hides true collision/target;
- charged water has clear state;
- Astrolabe and Leviathan reset completely;
- all three afterstates save/load;
- late join reconstructs correct water state;
- boss retry <20 seconds.
