# Iteration 3I — Periapsis Core & Final Configuration Production Brief

**Status:** Final major critical-path design pass  
**Scope:** Periapsis topology, Anchor Zero, final configuration paths, 1–4 player finale structure, world-state integration, faction/character closure, ending consequences, save/postgame policy and campaign-wide audit  
**Version 1 assumptions:** 2.5D, PC-first, solo + 1–4 online co-op, host-owned campaign state, granular accessibility

## Core promise

> **The player reconfigures the Meridian using everything they have learned and everything they have changed.**

Periapsis is not a final “ability exam” where every button is pressed once.

Its identity is **configuration under consequence**.

The region reads the player’s accumulated world state and asks them to stabilize an orbital habitat that now contains restored historical systems, transformed communities, adapted ecologies, distributed field intelligence, Custodian infrastructure and incompatible regional priorities.

## No new foundational ability

The critical toolkit is complete:

- Attract;
- Repel;
- Link / Echo;
- Latch;
- Orbit;
- Vector Shift;
- Flux;
- Phase;
- Invert.

Periapsis introduces new **configurations and combinations**, not new foundational controls.

## Final-region structure

### Act A — State Descent

The player enters Periapsis and sees accumulated campaign state expressed as machinery, support, hazards and route variants.

### Act B — Anchor Zero Reconfiguration

Anchor Zero is the major outer-layer infrastructure boss/system.

The goal is to bring the master Anchor into a neutral, controllable state.

### Act C — Configuration Forum

The player receives explicit, factual projections for available final configurations.

No ending is labeled good, bad, true or canon.

### Act D — Final Configuration Encounter

The final encounter changes mechanically and narratively by chosen configuration.

### Act E — Commit / Epilogue

The host confirms the irreversible final configuration after guest advisory vote.

Epilogue shows what survived, what was lost, what changed, which groups accepted or rejected the result, and what uncertainty remains.

## Final configuration set

### 1. Historical Continuity

Restoration-oriented.

Goal:

- re-center Anchor Zero;
- preserve high-capacity historical infrastructure;
- constrain uncontrolled field propagation.

Primary cost:

- some regional adaptations are overwritten or forced into narrower operating bands;
- Tessel communication is restricted;
- local autonomy decreases under centralized safety rules.

### 2. Distributed Meridian

Adaptation-oriented.

Goal:

- permanently distribute control among regional Anchor networks;
- preserve transformed societies/ecologies;
- prevent one master system from overwriting them.

Primary cost:

- historical high-capacity central systems are abandoned;
- some sectors remain permanently isolated or lower-power;
- full pre-fracture Meridian functionality can never be restored.

### 3. Open Resonance

Tessel/Communion-oriented.

Goal:

- integrate distributed field intelligence into Periapsis control;
- allow human and Tessel systems to co-regulate the habitat.

Primary cost:

- humans relinquish exclusive control;
- historical validation guarantees no longer apply;
- some communities opt out of field integration and lose access to integrated services;
- long-term Tessel behavior remains partly uncertain.

### 4. Bounded Accord

Advanced negotiated/hybrid configuration.

Goal:

- create a partitioned human/Tessel/distributed Anchor network with hard boundaries and regional autonomy.

This is **not** a golden ending.

Primary permanent cost:

- Anchor Zero is decommissioned as a future master controller;
- peak correction/energy reserve is reduced;
- the Meridian can never fully re-centralize;
- maintaining partitions requires permanent labor/infrastructure;
- some high-output historical functions are sacrificed.

## Availability philosophy

Historical Continuity and Distributed Meridian are always technically available after Anchor Zero.

Open Resonance requires a viable Tessel communication channel.

Bounded Accord requires several explicit support conditions, but every condition has more than one path to completion.

No ending depends on one undocumented missable collectible.

## Bounded Accord prerequisites

Displayed openly in Periapsis diagnostics:

1. **Controlled Field Channel**
   - Choir Partitioned Accord; or
   - Open Chorus + Safe Band/mediation work; or
   - Carrier Lock + restored bounded channel through late Tessel/Crown work.

2. **Distributed Support Capacity**
   - Cloud Loom Distributed Lift; or
   - sufficient regional support/calibration flags from multiple regions.

3. **Alignment Reserve**
   - Astral Shepherd completion; or
   - substitute calibration work across several regional Anchor diagnostics.

4. **Custodian Interface Access**
   - obtained through the mandatory Crown confrontation plus preserved diagnostic access.

The game tells the player which condition is missing and where a known route to it exists.

## World-state inputs

Periapsis consumes:

- Power Reserve;
- Water/Thermal Stability;
- Structural Access;
- Field Communication;
- Atmospheric Support;
- Habitat/Adaptation Support;
- Astral Shepherd Alignment Reserve;
- regional support/faction flags;
- key optional calibration flags.

These alter route difficulty, support and final costs without automatically deciding the ending.

## Player-count philosophy

### Solo

Echo supplies one simultaneous subsystem role.

### 2P

Paired subsystem responsibility.

### 3P

Pair + flexible stabilization/rescue role.

### 4P

Two pairs or four subsystem sectors.

Guests participate in all mechanics and advisory voting.

Host alone confirms irreversible campaign state.

## Finale difficulty

Challenge comes from system composition, responsibility, route reading, state changes and world-state consequences.

Avoid extreme HP, long runbacks, surprise input checks, mandatory builds and hidden ending logic.

## Save philosophy

Required save boundaries:

- Periapsis entry;
- before Anchor Zero;
- after Anchor Zero / before final configuration;
- branch-specific final encounter checkpoint;
- completion record after credits.

Before irreversible configuration, the game clearly states that the next commit determines the ending for this campaign snapshot.

## Postgame recommendation

Version 1 supports:

- a permanent completed-ending record;
- “Continue” returning to the **pre-final configuration save**;
- optional content/mastery cleanup;
- replaying another available configuration without replaying the whole campaign.

A fully transformed post-ending explorable Meridian and New Game+ are **not required Version 1 scope**.

## Exit criteria

Iteration 3I is complete when:

- Periapsis is greybox-ready;
- Anchor Zero is production-defined;
- all four final configurations have explicit mechanics and costs;
- prerequisites are discoverable;
- 1–4 player structures are defined;
- world-state integration is bounded;
- save/retry/postgame rules are locked;
- faction/Wayfarer closure is specified;
- finale network/performance risk is bounded;
- campaign-wide contradiction and soft-lock audit passes conceptually.
