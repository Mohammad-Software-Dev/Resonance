# Canonical Campaign Flow

**Status:** Iteration 3J source of truth for the Version 1 critical path  
**Purpose:** One end-to-end description from opening to credits

# Product frame

- 2.5D action-exploration Metroidvania.
- PC first.
- Solo + 1–4 player online co-op.
- One human per client.
- Four named Wayfarers: Mara Venn, Ily Sare, Jun Vale, Oren Kade.
- Host owns irreversible campaign state.
- Guests keep eligible personal progression/rewards.
- Challenging baseline with granular per-player accessibility assists.

# First 30 minutes

## Opening disaster

A Mass Anchor / Transit failure tears through the Meridian's transit infrastructure.

The active Wayfarer survives the failure and reaches Wayfarer Scar.

## First verbs

Player learns:

1. movement baseline;
2. Attract;
3. Repel;
4. basic combat;
5. first Resonance target/component interaction.

## Link / Echo introduction

Online party:

- learns Link;
- rescue/catch;
- cooperative force.

Solo:

- learns Echo as deterministic simultaneous-actor proxy.

The conceptual mechanic is the same in both modes.

## First local alignment repair

The player stabilizes an opening system.

Immediate lesson:

repairing one system can improve one condition while worsening another.

The game begins establishing its central question:

> What does “repair” mean when the world has already adapted to being broken?

# Opening arc — Wayfarer Scar / Transit Spine

Purpose:

- establish party/session rules;
- teach Resonance;
- introduce Relay;
- establish the Meridian as orbital habitat;
- open route choice.

Key milestone:

reach early Transit Spine and gain access toward Gravity Orchard / Solar Foundry outskirts.

# Region 1 — Gravity Orchard

## Major additions

- Latch;
- Orbit;
- first strongly systemic regional ecology;
- first major Anchor-state choice.

## Guardian

The Pendulum.

## Narrative role

First major proof that historical restoration, current inhabitants and adapted ecology may want different things.

## Exit

Orchard Anchor state transforms local/world routes.

This is the end of the production vertical slice target.

# Region 2 — Solar Foundry

## Major addition

Vector Shift.

## Region identity

- mirrors;
- solar transfer;
- molten glass;
- Heat;
- beam redirection.

## Guardian

The Glass Regent.

## Outcome role

Foundry decision contributes strongly to later **Power Reserve**.

# Region 3 — Flooded Observatory

## Major addition

No foundational movement ability.

This region deepens:

- momentum;
- media transitions;
- route reading;
- build mastery.

## Guardian

Leviathan of the Flood.

## Outcome role

Water/support decision contributes to **Water/Thermal Stability**.

# Region 4 — Rust Sea

## Major addition

Flux.

Core rule:

> Flux changes what can respond.

## Guardian

Dust Cathedral.

## Outcome role

Material migration/exposure contributes to **Structural Access**.

# Region 5 — Choir Array

## Major addition

Phase.

Core rule:

> Phase changes which field-state the player occupies.

## Guardian

Choir Prime.

## Narrative role

Tessel becomes undeniably intelligent/distributed, while identity and motives remain unresolved.

## Outcomes

- Carrier Lock;
- Open Chorus;
- Partitioned Accord.

Feeds **Field Communication**.

# Region 6 — Cloud Loom

## Major addition

Invert.

Core rule:

> Invert reverses a selected relationship.

## Guardian

Loom Harvester.

## Outcomes

- Maximum Harvest;
- Stable Envelope;
- Distributed Lift.

Feeds **Atmospheric Support** and power/resource state.

# Region 7 — Broken Transit Crown

## Function

Mastery/convergence region.

No new foundational ability.

The player revisits familiar Transit infrastructure after:

- detachment;
- rotation;
- prior regional transformations;
- Custodian occupation.

## Mandatory elite

Anchor Knight.

## Optional major encounter

Astral Shepherd.

Reward:

- Alignment Reserve;
- alternate route;
- mastery reward;
- Anchor Zero diagnostic.

Never required for finale.

## Mandatory major boss

Meridian Custodian.

Purpose:

- political/technical confrontation with Restoration argument;
- infrastructure control;
- adaptive but fair anti-pattern counterplay.

Victory opens Periapsis.

# Region 8 — Periapsis Core

## Entry

The game snapshots:

- regional choices;
- aggregate state;
- faction/support flags;
- Tessel channel state;
- optional Alignment Reserve.

## Shared subsystem petals

Periapsis expresses:

- Power;
- Water/Thermal;
- Structural;
- Field Communication;
- Atmospheric;
- Habitat/Adaptation.

## Mandatory boss

Anchor Zero.

Purpose:

bring the original master Anchor into **Neutral Zero**.

Boss victory does **not** choose the ending.

# Configuration Forum

After Anchor Zero, the player sees explicit projections for available configurations.

Always available:

- Historical Continuity;
- Distributed Meridian.

Conditional:

- Open Resonance with viable Tessel channel.

Advanced:

- Bounded Accord with explicit support prerequisites.

Guests vote/advice.

Host previews and ultimately confirms the irreversible choice.

# Final configuration encounter

Each path has distinct objective.

## Historical Continuity

Preserve central infrastructure while containing unstable field propagation.

## Distributed Meridian

Survive decentralization while safely allowing selected central systems to go offline.

## Open Resonance

Establish shared human/Tessel regulation under uncertainty.

## Bounded Accord

Build bounded partitions and permanently remove Anchor Zero's future master authority.

# Credits / postgame

After completion:

- configuration record persists;
- epilogue selects representative regional consequences;
- Continue returns to pre-final-commit state;
- player can finish optional content and complete another available configuration.

Version 1 does not require:

- four fully explorable post-ending worlds;
- New Game+;
- public matchmaking.

# Canonical campaign completion condition

A valid campaign always retains:

- a route to Periapsis;
- Historical Continuity;
- Distributed Meridian.

Conditional configurations add possibilities but never remove the ability to finish the game.
