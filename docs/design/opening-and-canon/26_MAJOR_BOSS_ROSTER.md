# Major Boss Roster — Canonical

**Canonical status:** Iteration 3J consolidated source of truth

## Boss philosophy

Major bosses must:

- express region/world fiction;
- use established Resonance rules in a new configuration;
- scale mechanically for 1–4 players;
- support solo Echo where simultaneous actors are required;
- avoid HP-only scaling;
- provide fast retries;
- preserve accessibility assists.

## Mandatory critical-path boss structure

### Opening mini-Guardian — Alignment Hound

**Region:** Wayfarer Scar  
**Status:** mandatory mini-Guardian, not counted as a full regional major boss.

Function:

- armor/component stripping;
- opposing force;
- first boss-like Resonance proof.

### 1. The Pendulum

**Region:** Gravity Orchard  
**Status:** mandatory major boss.

Identity:

- agricultural balancer;
- moving anchors;
- armor plates double as traversal surfaces;
- Orbit/strain mechanics.

### 2. The Glass Regent

**Region:** Solar Foundry  
**Status:** mandatory major boss.

Identity:

- mirrored fabrication intelligence;
- beams/mirrors;
- brittle armor;
- Heat/route management.

### 3. Leviathan of the Flood

**Region:** Flooded Observatory  
**Status:** mandatory major boss.

Identity:

- organism across free-floating water volumes;
- inside/outside sphere combat;
- water movement/momentum.

### 4. Dust Cathedral

**Region:** Rust Sea  
**Status:** mandatory major boss.

Identity:

- colony intelligence formed from metallic particles;
- terrain and boss draw from the same material pool;
- Flux exposes control knots.

### 5. Choir Prime

**Region:** Choir Array  
**Status:** mandatory major boss.

Identity:

- distributed network manifestation;
- signal routing;
- Phase;
- first undeniable Tessel self-preservation/communication.

### 6. Loom Harvester

**Region:** Cloud Loom  
**Status:** mandatory major boss.

Identity:

- moving atmospheric infrastructure;
- kite chase;
- intake/exhaust;
- Invert;
- sail/pressure control.

### 7. Meridian Custodian

**Region:** Broken Transit Crown  
**Status:** mandatory major boss.

Identity:

- human operator controlling infrastructure-scale systems;
- temporary anchors;
- route suppression;
- Link seizure;
- authored anti-pattern counters;
- Custodian political confrontation.

### 8. Anchor Zero

**Region:** Periapsis Core  
**Status:** mandatory major reconfiguration boss/system.

Identity:

- original master Mass Anchor;
- authored orientation rings;
- world-state-selected subsystem conflicts;
- Neutral Zero reconfiguration.

Boss victory does not choose the ending.

### 9. Final Configuration Encounter

**Region:** Periapsis Core  
**Status:** mandatory branch-specific finale after configuration commit.

This is one roster slot with **four mechanically distinct forms**, not four unrelated full games:

- Historical Continuity / Continuity Encounter;
- Distributed Meridian / Distribution Encounter;
- Open Resonance / Resonance Encounter;
- Bounded Accord / Accord Encounter.

Each expresses the cost of that configuration before completion.

## Optional major encounters

### Astral Shepherd

**Region:** Broken Transit Crown optional branch  
**Status:** optional major boss.

**Supersedes:** earlier descriptions that positioned Astral Shepherd as normal critical Crown progression.

Identity:

- station-scale alignment guardian;
- opposing force;
- articulated structural arms;
- floor-loss/aerial mastery.

Reward:

- Periapsis Alignment Reserve;
- alternate Crown route;
- high-tier mastery reward;
- Anchor Zero diagnostic.

Never gates the finale.

### Tessel Fragment — The Many-Voiced Gate

**Region:** late Choir/Core optional route  
**Status:** optional major encounter candidate.

Identity:

- defensive Tessel manifestation;
- hostile Echo-like traces;
- Link/Phase redirection;
- combat or negotiated shutdown depending quest state.

### Obsolete Anchor Prototype

**Old working name:** “The Pale Engine” — **deprecated; final name unresolved.**

**Region:** hidden Foundry/Spine junction  
**Status:** optional major/superboss candidate.

Identity:

- detects repeated movement routes;
- locks overused anchors;
- rewards improvisation.

The old working name must not ship.

## Mandatory boss pacing

Canonical late sequence:

Loom Harvester  
→ Anchor Knight elite  
→ Meridian Custodian  
→ Anchor Zero  
→ chosen Final Configuration Encounter

Astral Shepherd remains optional between Crown midpoint and Custodian.

## Difficulty bands

### Mandatory major bosses

- challenging;
- readable;
- fast retry;
- system learning over raw execution punishment;
- accessibility active.

### Optional major bosses

- stronger mastery expectations;
- tighter combinations;
- still readable/fair.

### Superbosses/challenges

May demand advanced mastery but cannot provide mandatory critical power.

## Player-count scaling

Scaling priority:

1. simultaneous responsibilities;
2. lane/subsystem count;
3. attack coverage;
4. modest durability;
5. HP last.

Solo uses Echo where multi-actor mechanics are conceptually required.

## Production target

The current canonical mandatory major-boss count is **9 major slots** plus the opening mini-Guardian, with Final Configuration Encounter branching into four forms.

Optional major encounters add breadth if production permits.

The older “12 mandatory major bosses” target is superseded by this structure.

This reduction protects boss quality, network reliability and late-game pacing.
