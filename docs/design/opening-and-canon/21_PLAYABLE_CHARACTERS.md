# Playable Characters — The Four Wayfarers

## Character design rule

All four characters share the **same essential locomotion, Resonance access, hitbox, progression gates and equipment system**. Character choice cannot make a route impossible.

Their differences come from:

- personality and story viewpoint;
- silhouette and animation flavor;
- starting module;
- one signature technique unlocked through personal quest progression;
- small side-grade passive that never creates mandatory party composition.

By midgame, players may acquire equivalent modules/techniques that cover most functional gaps. The purpose is identity, not class dependency.

# 1. Mara Venn — The Field Engineer

## Role in the ensemble

Mara is the practical center of the group and the first character most players naturally understand. She believes broken systems should be understood before they are restored.

## Personality

- methodical under pressure;
- dry humor;
- curious about machines;
- uncomfortable with dogma;
- tends to solve emotional problems like engineering problems.

## Visual identity

- asymmetrical ceramic work jacket over Resonance rig;
- heavier left forearm emitter;
- orange-white diagnostic light pattern;
- short utility cape/fabric strip that visibly reacts to force direction;
- visible face, practical hair, no mask.

## Starting module — Diagnostic Loop

After interacting with an enemy component or mechanism, Mara briefly receives clearer feedback about:

- mass class;
- loosened armor state;
- overload/Heat threshold;
- puzzle-node relationships.

This is informational, not extra damage.

## Signature technique — Field Brace

Mara can briefly create a stable temporary anchor on selected suitable surfaces.

Uses:

- recover from a dangerous launch;
- give teammates a temporary orbit point;
- stabilize a moving object during a puzzle;
- create a safe link point during a boss.

Cooldown/Heat cost prevents replacing authored anchors.

## Personal arc

Mara's former engineering guild taught that the Meridian had one correct configuration. Her story is about accepting that maintenance can mean adaptation rather than restoration.

# 2. Ily Sare — The Cartographic Ecologist

## Role in the ensemble

Ily studies how life changed after the Meridian fractured. They are the strongest early advocate for treating new ecosystems as legitimate rather than defective.

## Personality

- observant;
- empathetic but stubborn;
- fascinated by strange life;
- impatient with people who treat maps as fixed truth;
- tells stories about places rather than dates.

## Visual identity

- layered survey harness;
- translucent map/projector elements;
- teal-gold field markers;
- flexible botanical sample pouches and sensor rods;
- graceful, low-profile animation language.

## Starting module — Living Survey

Ily gains stronger environmental cues around:

- hidden ecological routes;
- migrating creatures;
- recently transformed habitats;
- certain secret entrances indicated by living behavior.

It does not automatically reveal secrets on the map.

## Signature technique — Trail Mark

Ily can place one temporary Resonance waypoint that teammates/Echoes can target more easily and that improves rescue/Link acquisition near it.

Useful for:

- coordinated traversal;
- rally points;
- teaching a less experienced player a route;
- boss positioning.

## Personal arc

Ily begins convinced preservation is always morally superior. Their arc confronts cases where an adapted ecosystem is itself destroying another population.

# 3. Jun Vale — The Transit Runner

## Role in the ensemble

Jun is a former high-speed courier who crossed unstable Meridian sectors before official routes reopened. They embody movement mastery and social warmth.

## Personality

- impulsive;
- playful;
- competitive;
- unusually good at reading people;
- masks fear with jokes, but not constant quips.

## Visual identity

- streamlined rig;
- elongated conductive rails along legs/forearms;
- magenta-white acceleration streaks;
- scarf/ribbon element designed to exaggerate speed;
- athletic but not superhero proportions.

## Starting module — Carry Momentum

Jun loses slightly less horizontal speed after selected attacks and Link releases.

The bonus is deliberately modest; anyone can master fast routes.

## Signature technique — Vector Kick

After a well-timed Repel release, Jun can redirect a portion of momentum once without consuming the standard Vector Shift charge.

Useful for:

- advanced traversal;
- rescue corrections;
- aerial combo positioning.

## Personal arc

Jun's old identity depends on being the person who can always reach anyone. Their story challenges the belief that being useful means never needing rescue themselves.

# 4. Oren Kade — The Custodian Defector

## Role in the ensemble

Oren once served the Custodian Assembly, the institution most committed to restoring the Meridian's original configuration. He knows procedures and secrets the others do not.

## Personality

- reserved;
- formal when nervous;
- deeply loyal once trust is earned;
- carries guilt over earlier restoration operations;
- surprisingly funny when rules break down.

## Visual identity

- repurposed ceremonial maintenance armor;
- broad shoulder geometry without becoming a tank silhouette;
- cool white/violet field lattice;
- visible former-Custodian insignia physically scratched out;
- deliberate, economical movement animation.

## Starting module — Stability Protocol

Oren recovers slightly faster from heavy knockback/stability break and provides a small Link-stability benefit when bracing with a teammate.

## Signature technique — Counterfield

A precisely timed Repel against a heavy Resonant attack creates a short-lived counterfield that teammates can use as an anchor/projectile redirect point.

## Personal arc

Oren knows that some past “repairs” displaced or killed communities. His arc is about whether responsibility means restoring trust, exposing the truth or abandoning the institution entirely.

# Party relationship matrix

## Mara ↔ Ily

Conflict: engineering certainty versus ecological adaptation.  
Bond: both are obsessive observers.  
Arc: learn to distinguish “what can be repaired” from “what should be restored.”

## Mara ↔ Jun

Conflict: planning versus improvisation.  
Bond: both love elegant mechanical solutions.  
Arc: Mara learns to trust experimentation; Jun learns that planning enables bigger risks.

## Mara ↔ Oren

Conflict: Mara distrusts Custodian doctrine.  
Bond: technical competence and responsibility.  
Arc: mutual respect depends on Oren confronting institutional harm honestly.

## Ily ↔ Jun

Conflict: Jun moves too quickly through places Ily wants to understand.  
Bond: curiosity and affection for ordinary people.  
Arc: Jun begins treating places as communities, not routes.

## Ily ↔ Oren

Conflict: strongest ideological tension.  
Bond: both care intensely about continuity and memory.  
Arc: becomes one of the deepest friendships if Oren supports adaptation.

## Jun ↔ Oren

Conflict: irreverence versus procedure.  
Bond: each is willing to take personal risks for others.  
Arc: Jun helps Oren become socially open; Oren helps Jun accept limits.

# Multiplayer character rules

## Duplicate characters

Recommendation: in story campaign, a party normally uses unique characters. If two players select the same character in quick-play/challenge modes, allow duplicates for convenience, but canonical story scenes treat the four as unique individuals.

If campaign matchmaking must allow duplicates, present duplicates as simulation/Relay echoes outside story scenes. This needs testing; simpler is unique-character reservation within a campaign party.

## Character switching

At major Relays/settlements, players may switch to another available Wayfarer without losing personal profile progression.

In a persistent shared campaign, quest dialogue adapts to active characters but critical information is never permanently missable.

## Banter frequency

Banter triggers on:

- first region entry;
- major transformation;
- selected discoveries;
- character quest beats;
- rare rescue/combat events.

Avoid commentary every minute. Silence must remain part of the atmosphere.

# Character progression

Each character has a 3-step personal quest across the campaign. Completion unlocks:

1. signature technique;
2. cosmetic/animation variant;
3. final character-scene resolution and an optional module usable by all characters.

This preserves personality while preventing power from being permanently locked to one favorite.
