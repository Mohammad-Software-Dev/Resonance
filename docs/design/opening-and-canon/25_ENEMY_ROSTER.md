# Enemy Roster and Encounter Families — Canonical

**Canonical status:** Iteration 3J consolidated source of truth

## Roster philosophy

The game favors meaningful archetypes over palette swaps.

Current full design contains roughly **45–50 standard archetype slots** across reusable and regional families before production cuts.

Launch target may consolidate some overlapping roles toward approximately **40–44 strong archetypes**.

A variant counts as a new archetype only if behavior or decision-making materially changes.

## Broken Transit Crown / Custodian

The old provisional names **Custodian Runner, Brace Guard, Field Auditor, Relay Seizer and Protocol Drone are superseded**.

| Enemy | Role | Resonance hook |
|---|---|---|
| Route Surveyor | observation/support | marks repeated route behavior |

| Phase Warden | state control | local Phase seal/projector |
| Stabilizer | heavy control | force-resistant brace relation |

**Elite:** Anchor Knight.

Custodian counters are:

- semantic-event driven;
- telegraphed;
- temporary;
- unable to remove every safe route.

## Periapsis

Periapsis intentionally introduces minimal new standard enemy families.

It remixes:

- earlier machines/fauna;
- Custodian systems;
- Tessel manifestations;
- world-state hazards;
- configuration-specific scripted manifestations.

The final hours prioritize system recombination over a fresh bestiary.

## Player-count encounter scaling

### Solo

- fewer simultaneous hard tracking threats;
- Echo-safe setup windows;
- smaller support combinations.

### 2P

- paired pressure;
- cross-arena target switching.

### 3P

- additional support/objective responsibility;
- arena spacing prevents dog-piling.

### 4P

- multiple simultaneous lanes/objectives;
- support/control enemies become more important;
- modest durability scaling;
- multi-target patterns;
- simplified friendly/decorative VFX where necessary.

## AI targeting

Target selection may weight:

- recent damage;
- position/role;
- active Link user;
- objective carrier;
- isolated player;
- mechanic assignment.

Target swaps must be readable.

## Introduction cadence

1. introduce archetype alone/simple pair;
2. combine with complementary role;
3. use in mixed encounter;
4. remix on transformed return.

## Production cut rule

If enemy scope must shrink:

Cut or merge archetypes with overlapping decision roles before cutting:

- foundational Resonance teaching enemies;
- one strong regional identity enemy set;
- major elite preparation mechanics;
- Custodian counterplay family.

Do not reduce scope through palette swaps presented as new content.
