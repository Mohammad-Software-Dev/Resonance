# World Topology and Region States — Canonical

**Canonical status:** Iteration 3J consolidated source of truth

## Setting

The Meridian is a continent-scale orbital habitat around a gas giant.

Its regions are connected by damaged ring infrastructure, Transit Spine routes, Mass Anchor systems and later field/atmospheric cross-links.

The game is interconnected rather than a linear level list, but progression still has a readable major sequence.

## Region sequence and purpose

### 0. Wayfarer Scar

Purpose:

- opening disaster;
- Attract/Repel;
- Link/Echo;
- named party introduction;
- first local alignment repair.

Later returns show enclave growth, Phase archive route and late mastery spaces.

### 1. Transit Spine

Purpose:

- connective tissue;
- early route choice;
- Relay/party infrastructure;
- recurring recognizable architecture.

Late game:

upper Spine sectors detach/rotate into **Broken Transit Crown**.

### 2. Gravity Orchard

Identity:

- agricultural cylinder;
- fractured gravity/ecology;
- Latch/Orbit;
- first major proof that “repair” and “restore” are not identical.

Guardian:

**The Pendulum**

Regional outcome families:

- historical/standard stabilization;
- preserve fractured ecology;
- hybrid calibration if prerequisites are completed.

Exact implementation is defined by the detailed Orchard production files.

### 3. Solar Foundry

Identity:

- mirrors;
- molten glass;
- solar/energy transfer;
- Vector Shift.

Guardian:

**The Glass Regent**

Regional decision influences:

- central power;
- civic/local access;
- experimental/Fractured Spectrum infrastructure.

Canonical late aggregate contribution:

**Power Reserve** and related support state.

### 4. Flooded Observatory

Identity:

- free-floating water spheres;
- lens/research habitats;
- momentum through media;
- no foundational movement unlock.

Guardian:

**Leviathan of the Flood**

Regional choices determine historic channels, suspended/adapted water state, or relief/support diversion.

Canonical late aggregate contribution:

**Water/Thermal Stability** and support state.

### 5. Rust Sea

Identity:

- metallic particulate ocean over machine-city;
- Flux;
- material migration/exposure.

Guardian:

**Dust Cathedral**

Canonical regional outcome families include:

- excavation/exposure;
- stable particulate tides;
- directed migration/redistribution.

Canonical late aggregate contribution:

**Structural Access** and material support.

### 6. Choir Array

Identity:

- communication towers;
- standing-wave networks;
- Phase;
- first undeniable Tessel evidence.

Guardian:

**Choir Prime**

Regional outcomes:

- Carrier Lock;
- Open Chorus;
- Partitioned Accord.

Canonical late aggregate contribution:

**Field Communication**.

### 7. Cloud Loom

Identity:

- atmospheric harvesting;
- kites/sails;
- deterministic current lanes;
- Invert;
- gas-giant exterior traversal.

Guardian:

**Loom Harvester**

Regional outcomes:

- Maximum Harvest;
- Stable Envelope;
- Distributed Lift.

Canonical late aggregate contribution:

**Atmospheric Support** and power/resource state.

### 8. Broken Transit Crown

Identity:

- transformed familiar Transit geography;
- Custodian occupation;
- full-toolkit mastery;
- no new foundational ability.

Mandatory elite:

**Anchor Knight**

Mandatory major boss:

**Meridian Custodian**

Optional major encounter:

**Astral Shepherd**

Crown derives visible state from all prior regional outcomes.

### 9. Periapsis Core

Identity:

- final state convergence;
- authored orientation rings;
- Anchor Zero;
- final configuration.

Mandatory boss/system:

**Anchor Zero**

Final configuration families:

- Historical Continuity;
- Distributed Meridian;
- Open Resonance;
- Bounded Accord.

## Campaign ordering freedom

### Opening

Wayfarer Scar → Transit Spine.

### Early

Gravity Orchard and Solar Foundry outskirts may be approached with limited ordering freedom.

The opening production slice canonically culminates in The Pendulum / first major Anchor-state decision.

### Mid

Solar Foundry completion, Flooded Observatory, Rust Sea and return routes progressively interconnect.

### Late-mid

Choir Array becomes accessible after sufficient infrastructure progression.

### Late

Cloud Loom → Broken Transit Crown → Periapsis Core.

Optional major encounters and old-region returns may occur around this sequence.

## Cross-region consequence rule

Every major regional outcome must affect at least one other region or late aggregate state.

Consequences should appear through:

- route changes;
- power/water/material support;
- NPC relocation;
- ecology;
- field communication;
- current/weather;
- late-game support.

## Canonical late aggregate state

Periapsis/Crown use these bounded derived groups:

- Power Reserve;
- Water/Thermal Stability;
- Structural Access;
- Field Communication;
- Atmospheric Support;
- Habitat/Adaptation Support.

Additional explicit support flags include:

- Astral Shepherd Alignment Reserve;
- regional quest/calibration flags;
- faction/Tessel interface flags.

There is no hidden morality score.

## Fast travel

Relays are the primary fast-travel network.

Early:

- Relay-to-Relay.

Mid:

- safe return to nearest Relay where specified.

Late:

- transformed regional shortcuts;
- selected field/Phase/current routes.

Fast travel never replaces meaningful first traversal.

## Region size rule

Detailed production specs override the older generic “25–40 rooms each” guideline.

Current regions have individually specified room budgets.

When scope is cut:

- remove optional density first;
- preserve region identity;
- preserve critical route;
- preserve major boss;
- preserve world transformation.

## Final world-state rule

Legal regional choices may change:

- route quality;
- hazard type;
- support;
- optional content;
- final configuration cost/availability.

They may not remove all critical routes or make the campaign unwinnable.
