# Progression, Loadouts and Economy

## Progression philosophy

Progression should expand expression more often than it increases raw numbers.

The player becomes stronger through four channels:

1. **Major Resonance abilities** — change where/how the player can move and interact.
2. **Cores** — define broad playstyle emphasis.
3. **Modules** — smaller behavior modifications and synergies.
4. **Capacity upgrades** — health, Heat tolerance, repair charges, etc.

Avoid conventional character levels and random rarity loot.

## Major abilities

Suggested progression set:

- Attract — opening.
- Repel — opening.
- Latch — early.
- Link — early-mid; immediately in multiplayer, deeper functions unlocked over time.
- Orbit — early-mid.
- Echo — early for solo; tutorialized separately.
- Vector Shift — mid.
- Flux Field — mid.
- Phase — late-mid.
- Invert — late.
- Overdrive — endgame mastery state, possibly optional.

Every major ability must unlock:

- traversal opportunity;
- combat interaction;
- at least one puzzle expression;
- at least one boss interaction.

## Cores

Cores are broad equipment identities, not fixed classes.

### Vector Core

Focus: movement/momentum.

Possible effects:

- retains more speed after attacks;
- stronger Snap Launch;
- aerial momentum strike;
- reduced Heat on movement techniques.

### Breaker Core

Focus: stability/armor.

- faster component cracking;
- heavy strikes transfer more force;
- bonus utility against heavy objects;
- armor removal creates shockwave.

### Weaver Core

Focus: Links/co-op/control.

- longer Link range;
- Link can briefly bind light enemies;
- force transmission efficiency;
- improved rescue windows.

### Ghost Core

Focus: Phase/evasion.

- Phase counterattack;
- longer but more costly phase;
- pass through selected projectiles to charge Resonance.

### Conduit Core

Focus: projectiles/energy.

- better catches/reflections;
- store one captured projectile class;
- chain energy through Resonant targets.

### Mender Core

Focus: survivability/support.

- improved Fracture recovery;
- faster teammate stabilization;
- convert perfect defense to nearby support pulse.

All players can switch Cores at Relays. No one is trapped as “the healer.”

## Modules

Modules are smaller modifiers installed into limited slots/capacity.

Good module examples:

- Repel after perfect reflection creates micro-launch.
- Orbiting for a minimum duration charges next heavy attack.
- Removing armor returns a small amount of Heat.
- Rescue Link briefly increases both players' stability.
- Phase through an attack preserves Fracture timer.
- Flux lasts longer but generates more Heat.

Bad module pattern:

- +2% generic damage;
- +1.3% movement speed;
- random rarity duplicates.

Numbers may exist for tuning, but modules should be understood as behaviors.

## Capacity model

A Core has a **Load Capacity**. Modules cost 1–3 capacity depending on impact.

Players unlock more capacity through exploration, but not enough to equip everything.

This creates builds without directly copying charm/notch structures visually or thematically. Use circuit channels / rig bandwidth as the fiction and create an original UI arrangement.

## Health progression

Health grows slowly and predictably.

Methods:

- complete major Anchor milestones;
- assemble rare structural upgrades from exploration;
- optional challenge rewards.

Avoid dozens of tiny health fragments unless world fiction strongly supports them. Fewer, more meaningful upgrades are easier to understand.

## Resonance Heat progression

Players may improve:

- maximum safe Heat;
- cooling rate;
- efficiency for selected actions.

Basic movement remains free enough to avoid stamina frustration.

## Economy

Use a small number of currencies/resources.

### Salvage

Common world resource used for:

- module calibration/unlocks;
- map services;
- cosmetic rig changes;
- selected settlement contributions.

Retained on death.

### Anchor Shards

Rare progression material from major exploration/elite challenges.

Used for meaningful capacity or system upgrades. Limited enough that spending can matter but generous enough to avoid build lock-in.

### Faction Tokens / Favor

Prefer reputation tracked through actions/quests rather than another collectible currency. Where possible, unlock through choices instead of grinding mobs.

## Shops

Merchants should sell information and playstyle options, not mandatory power gates.

Possible stock:

- Modules.
- Maps/region annotations.
- Cosmetics.
- Optional challenge keys/signals.
- Lore objects.
- Limited utility refills.

Stock can change after world transformations.

## Quest rewards

Prioritize:

- changed world state;
- new NPC presence;
- module/core variant;
- shortcut or service;
- lore with gameplay consequence;
- cosmetic trophy.

## Co-op progression ownership

General principle:

- major abilities should synchronize enough that guests are never mechanically stranded;
- permanent personal unlocks belong to each player's profile;
- host world decisions remain in host save unless a shared-campaign mode is explicitly chosen;
- guests receive credit for eligible bosses/objectives so replay burden is minimized.

Detailed rules in `12_MULTIPLAYER_NETWORK_SAVE_MATCHMAKING.md`.

## Respec

Build experimentation should be cheap.

At any Relay:

- switch Core;
- swap Modules;
- save loadout presets later in game.

No currency fee for ordinary reconfiguration.

## Completion rewards

Endgame rewards should support mastery:

- advanced traversal modules;
- alternate Core techniques;
- cosmetic rig transformations;
- challenge modifiers;
- map completion tools;
- superboss access.

Avoid “New Game+ only to get +50% stats” as the primary postgame incentive.
